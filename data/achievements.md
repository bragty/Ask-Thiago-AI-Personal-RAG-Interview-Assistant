- Worked with clinical coding data and ICD-10 diagnosis structures.
- Applied exact-match and semantic similarity evaluation.
- Helped produce quantitative evidence about model performance.
- Supported the interpretation of AI results in a healthcare context.

### Important Results

- Top-5 exact match for main diagnoses: 45.57%

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Evaluation at Kantonsspital Aarau

Thiago worked on evaluating Microsoft 365 Copilot use cases at Kantonsspital Aarau.

The evaluation focused on productivity impact, time savings, quality, user pain points, and potential economic value.

### Key Achievements

- Evaluated practical Copilot use cases in a hospital organization.
- Compared task completion with and without AI support.
- Measured time savings and quality.
- Considered usability, data protection concerns, and workflow fit.
- Analyzed user feedback and AI limitations.
- Identified possible AI agent ideas for internal hospital workflows.

### Example Result

One evaluated use case involved completing an Excel-based name and department task.

Measured result:

- Human completion time: 628 seconds
- AI-supported completion time: 115 seconds
- Time savings: 81.7%
- Error count: 0 out of 24 for both workflows
- Estimated cost reduction at 100 CHF/hour: from 17.46 CHF to 3.20 CHF
- Estimated break-even: 1.77 tasks per month

This achievement shows that Thiago can evaluate AI tools not only technically, but also from an organizational and business perspective. He understands that AI solutions need measurable value, usability, and workflow fit.
